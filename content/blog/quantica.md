Quântica is a *4-channel sequencing quantizer* designed to make building chord progressions fast and intuitive in Eurorack systems.

> images 700*500

## This Machine Quantizes
> In + Gate -> Brain -> Quantized output
Each channel has a CV input with selectable range. The value is mapped to the nearest active note, and sent to the channel's output. A gate input provides sample & hold control.
CV and gate inputs are normaled downwards, allowing you to control multiple devices with fewer inputs.

## This Machine Sequences
> Sequencer background
Quântica's inbuilt chord sequencer lets you record and playback patterns of up to 8 steps. The sequencer can be triggered by an external clock signal, with a configurable clock divider.

## This Machine Arpeggiates
> arp background
Each channel has an arpeggiator which is applied to the output note after quantization. Use a CV input to control the inversion of the chord, or use the normalisation to create more complex patterns

## This Machine Puts Music Theory At Your Fingertips
> Music theory / progression wheel background
With an inbuilt knowledge of key and chord structure, Quântica makes it easy and fast to find chords which fit into your chosen key.

## This Machine Guides, Without Restriction
> Chord Map background
Quântica's Chord Map uses an intelligent chord suggestion engine, to help you on your musical journey. Suggestions are just that however, you are free to go as far afield as you want.
